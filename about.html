About us

We bake more than bread — we deliver memorable moments.

Comrade Choice Bakery began as a kitchen dream to bring fresh, handcrafted baked goods to the heart of Eldoret. Today we serve pastries, specialty breads, custom cakes, and warm beverages made from premium ingredients every single day.

Our team combines classic techniques with creative flavors so every order feels personal and made to celebrate your moments. Whether you are preparing for a meeting, a birthday, or a cozy weekend treat, our bakery is ready to make it special.
